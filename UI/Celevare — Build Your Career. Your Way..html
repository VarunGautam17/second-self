The Process

How Celevare Works

A structured path from where you are to where you want to be.

01

Choose Your Path

Select AI-powered tools, work with a human expert, or combine both. There's no wrong choice — only the one that fits your situation and timeline.

02

Build or Upload Your Resume

Start from scratch, choose a template, or upload your existing resume. Our AI begins optimizing immediately regardless of your starting point.

03

Optimize with AI or Experts

AI clients get instant ATS analysis against real job descriptions. Expert clients work directly with their writer to refine every word of their narrative.

04

Review & Approve

Review your score, keyword alignment, and formatting. Make targeted edits or collaborate with your expert until you're completely satisfied.

05

Apply with Confidence

Export in multiple formats, tailor to specific roles, and apply knowing your materials are competitive at the highest level of your industry.
